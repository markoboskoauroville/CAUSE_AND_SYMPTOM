# Illustration Prompt Book — *Cause and Symptom*
### Nano Banana generation sheet · pencil-and-photo split method

---

## 0. The Method

Same principle as *The Brain Brake*: **two registers in one frame.**

- **PENCIL REGISTER** — graphite sketch on off-white paper, visible tooth of the paper, construction lines left in, light smudging, occasional eraser ghost. This register always carries the **CAUSE**: the principle, the mechanism, the thing that is not visible in the world.
- **PHOTO REGISTER** — photoreal, natural light, documentary framing, no styling, no stock-photo gloss. This register always carries the **SYMPTOM**: the visible surface, the thing everyone sees and argues about.

The two registers must **share one continuous space**. Never a diptych with a border. The pencil should bleed into the photograph, or the photograph should emerge out of the pencil, so the eye reads one image and only afterwards notices there are two kinds of truth in it.

**Fixed rules for every prompt in this book:**

1. Palette: near-black, warm graphite grey, off-white paper, and one warm gold accent (#E8B15C) used **only** on the causal element — never decoratively.
2. No text, no labels, no arrows with words, no infographic look.
3. No faces of identifiable real people. No flags. No party symbols. No recognisable politicians.
4. For war and migration chapters: **no bodies, no blood, no wounded, no children in distress.** These chapters are illustrated by structure and absence, never by suffering. This is not squeamishness — a photograph of grief is itself symptom-level, and the book argues against that reflex.
5. Aspect: 3:2 landscape for chapter openers, 1:1 for the character sheets.

**Workflow:** generate character sheets first → keep the best of each → feed them back as reference for chapter frames so the visual language stays continuous → drop finished images into the slots marked below.

## 1. CHARACTER SHEETS
*Generate these first. They are the visual vocabulary everything else is built from.*

### CS-01 — THE CHAIN
> Character sheet on off-white paper. A single physical chain of five heavy iron links laid diagonally across the sheet, drawn in graphite pencil with visible construction lines and light smudging. The first link on the left is rendered in warm gold, faint, almost drawn in light. The last link on the right is rendered photorealistically as an actual heavy rusted iron link, with real texture, real shadow, real corrosion, as though a photograph has been pasted into the drawing at that end. The transition between drawn and photographed happens gradually across the middle three links. Beside the chain, small studies of the same chain from three other angles, sketch quality. No text.

### CS-02 — THE PATIENT
> Character sheet on off-white paper. Studies of one middle-aged human figure, gender neutral, ordinary build, ordinary clothes, seen from four angles: standing, seated with hands on knees, lying, walking. Drawn in graphite with visible construction lines. In one of the four studies only, the figure is rendered as a real photograph, natural window light, tired ordinary face, no styling, no drama. The other three remain pencil. A faint warm gold line runs from the figure's chest outward past the edge of the paper in all four studies, as if something continues beyond the frame. No text.

### CS-03 — THE TWO HANDS
> Character sheet on off-white paper. Two hands, studied repeatedly. The left hand is drawn in graphite reaching with an open palm, slow, deliberate, fingers relaxed. The right hand is photographed, real skin, real light, gripping a small object tightly, tendons visible, fast and decisive. Six studies total, alternating drawn and photographed, arranged as an anatomy study sheet. Warm gold appears only as a faint glow at the base of the open drawn palm. No text.

### CS-04 — THE FIELD
> Character sheet on off-white paper. Studies of a wheat field. Three drawn studies in graphite: one of a single wheat stalk with its root system exposed, one of a mixed hedgerow with many species layered, one of an insect. One photoreal study occupying the lower right: an endless flat monoculture wheat field under a hard flat sky, photographed documentary style, absolutely uniform to the horizon, no hedge, no tree, no bird. Warm gold appears only in the drawn root system. No text.

### CS-05 — THE RIVER
> Character sheet on off-white paper. Studies of water motion. Three drawn studies in graphite: a spiral vortex seen from above, a meandering river bend seen in plan with its inner and outer bank, and a cross-section of a natural riverbed shaped like an egg. One photoreal study: a straightened concrete channel, grey, rectangular, water moving fast and flat, banks poured and lifeless, photographed in overcast light. Warm gold traces only the spiral of the drawn vortex. No text.

### CS-06 — THE THRESHOLD
> Character sheet on off-white paper. Studies of a horizon line where water meets land. Four drawn graphite studies of an empty shoreline at different times of day, no people, no boats. One photoreal study of the same empty shoreline, real light, real stones, real foam, entirely deserted. A single faint warm gold line runs horizontally through every study at the same height, continuous across all five, as though one measurement connects them. No text.

### CS-07 — THE ARCHITECTURE OF AN AGREEMENT
> Character sheet on off-white paper. Studies of an empty negotiating table seen from four angles, drawn in graphite, no chairs occupied, no people, no flags, no papers. One photoreal study of a real empty conference room, fluorescent light, chairs pushed in, a jug of water, nothing else. Warm gold appears only along the drawn edge of the table in the pencil studies, like a seam. No text.

## 3. INSERTION PROTOCOL

When an image is generated and approved:

1. Save as the exact filename in the slot above, into `docs/img/`.
2. Change `[ ] pending` to `[x] done` in this file.
3. The image is inserted at the **top of its chapter**, directly under the chapter heading, in both language versions.
4. Rebuild the site with `build_site.py` — image slots that are still pending are simply skipped, so the site can be rebuilt at any stage.

**Caption convention:** none. The images carry no text and no caption. If an image needs explaining, it is the wrong image.

---

## 4. NOTES FOR GENERATION

- Nano Banana holds character consistency well when given a reference image; always attach the relevant character sheet from section 1 when generating a chapter frame.
- The hardest thing to get is the **bleed** between registers. If the model produces a hard split, add: *the drawn and photographed areas must share one continuous surface, with the paper grain visible faintly through the photographic region, no border, no frame, no division line.*
- If the gold reads as decorative rather than causal, add: *the gold appears on one element only and that element is the cause; everything else is graphite, black, and off-white.*
- Resist the temptation to illustrate suffering. Every chapter in this book argues that the visible distressing thing is the least informative part of the picture. The images should argue the same.
