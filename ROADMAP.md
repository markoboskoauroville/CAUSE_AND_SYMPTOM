# Roadmap to 300 pages

**Page convention used throughout: 300 words = 1 page.** That is a standard trade
paperback density (Georgia 11pt, generous leading, the format the site already
uses). Front matter, part titles and illustration pages are not counted; only body
text is. Illustrations, once generated, add roughly one page each.

Counts are reported after every expansion round.

## Expansion slots

Ordered by how much each strengthens the spine rather than by length. Nothing here
is padding; each slot exists because the argument currently has a hole in it.

### Priority A — the argument needs these

**A1. Reading the recovery literature.** ~10 pages. Survivorship bias, regression to
the mean, co-intervention, diagnostic uncertainty. Sorting the popular healing books
into what they can and cannot establish. Currently the book asserts that testimony
proves possibility rather than frequency and never demonstrates it.

**A2. The economy: debt as symptom.** ~12 pages. Interest-rate policy and stimulus as
analgesia; productivity, demography and distribution as the chain beneath. The purest
non-biological example of a rising dose, and the one where "the exit condition was
never written down" is documented in central bank minutes.

**A3. Addiction.** ~12 pages. The hardest case for the whole framework, because the
substance is simultaneously symptom, cause and treatment. Where the triage rule is
most contested — harm reduction versus abstinence is precisely the argument this
book is about.

**A4. The mind: psychotherapy as cause-work.** ~14 pages. Symptom-focused versus
depth approaches, what each is good for, why the profession has the same split as
medicine, and the honest evidence on both sides.

### Priority B — deepens what exists

**B1. Iatrogenesis and overdiagnosis.** ~10 pages. Expanding Chapter 3: how expanding
diagnostic thresholds manufacture patients, and how harm distributes across
populations who cannot benefit.

**B2. The city.** ~10 pages. Traffic, congestion charges and induced demand — the
cleanest available proof that adding capacity to relieve a symptom regenerates it.

**B3. Fire.** ~10 pages. A century of suppression producing the fuel load for
catastrophe. Possibly the single most literal illustration of the whole book.

**B4. Education and the test.** ~10 pages. Measuring an outcome and then teaching to
the measure — the symptom becoming the target, formally.

**B5. Soil and water in the Mediterranean.** ~10 pages. Karst, erosion, terracing,
and the specific case of an island restoration project. Local, concrete, first-hand.

### Priority C — structural

**C1. Objections and replies.** ~14 pages. The strongest arguments *against* this
book, answered honestly: that "cause" is infinitely regressive; that cause-work is a
luxury of the comfortable; that the fractal is pattern-matching rather than mechanism.
A book without this chapter is advocacy.

**C2. Case studies, worked end to end.** ~20 pages. Four or five real trajectories
followed through the method in Chapter 16, showing where it works and where it
breaks.

**C3. History of the idea.** ~12 pages. Hippocratic constitution, Galen, the
miasma–contagion argument, Virchow on social medicine, Selye, Antonovsky, Illich.
The argument is old and the book should say so.

**C4. Glossary, index, bibliography.** ~14 pages.

## Arithmetic

Current 40 + Priority A (48) + Priority B (50) + Priority C (60) = **198 pages**,
plus 18 illustrations ≈ 216. Reaching 300 means either expanding the strongest
existing chapters — several are currently compressed to two or three pages where
they deserve eight — or adding a fifth domain part.

**The honest recommendation: do not target 300 as such.** Target the argument being
complete, then count. A padded 300 is worth less than a tight 220. If the count still
falls short once every slot above is filled, the right move is depth in Parts II and
III rather than new domains, because the fractal is already demonstrated and further
domains would be repetition.
